About us

A service company at its clients’ service

Numarsi was founded in 2011 to meet the need of many fish processors and distributors to outsource part of their production processes.

Contract processing

We offer contract processing services to companies that need their products made. We are a service company that complements our clients’ production processes and forms part of their value chain. Today we have two factories totalling 3,500 m² and a team of more than 180 people.

What sets us apart

What makes us an ally for our clients.

Processing specialists

Quality philosophy

High quality and food safety standards, with a Quality Department that checks the whole process.

Extra services and deadlines
