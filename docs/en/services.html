Services

We adapt to each client’s needs

While preserving the quality of their raw materials. Book each service separately or combine them to receive your product finished, frozen and packed.

Fish filleting and skinning

We work from two kinds of raw material. Respect for the raw material, precision and meticulous work are our strengths.

  • From headed and gutted (HG) fish: filleting, or filleting and skinning
  • From fresh or IQF fillets: skinning

Cephalopod cleaning, gutting and treatment

We have a dedicated line for gutting and then cutting into rings.

  • Dedicated line for gutting and cutting into rings
  • Chilled rooms for any treatments required
  • Subsequent storage

Cutting in different sizes and shapes

We offer a wide range of cuts, in both shape and size, with a highly skilled team and automatic equipment.

  • Medallions, steaks, cubes and strips, among others
  • Automatic portioning from fish blocks
  • A wide variety of shapes and sizes

IQF freezing and glazing

We have two dynamic freezing tunnels and two drying tunnels with a capacity of 4 t/h, where we freeze and glaze.

  • Two dynamic freezing tunnels and two drying tunnels
  • Capacity of 4 tonnes per hour
  • Freezing and glazing
  • Tunnels strategically placed in the plant for greater overall efficiency

Packing in different sizes and formats

We pack in the size and format each client needs.

  • Bags
  • Double skin
  • Vacuum packs
  • Cartons
  • Bulk
